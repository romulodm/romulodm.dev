<threat_model>
- **T-1-04:** PR checks are so broad or fragile that they are disabled, bypassed, or left pending.
- **T-1-05:** Local and GitHub CI drift apart, creating false confidence about passing checks.
- **T-1-06:** Legacy surfaces are silently excluded from baseline validation even though they remain public.

Mitigations required in this plan:
- Define one root CI contract in `package.json`.
- Keep Phase 1 checks lightweight and deterministic.
- Scope jobs intentionally but avoid required-workflow designs that remain pending when filtered out.
</threat_model>
