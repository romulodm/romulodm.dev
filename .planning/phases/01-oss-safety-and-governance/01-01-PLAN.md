<threat_model>
- **T-1-01:** A public push exposes real credentials or session state because ignore rules or tracked-file audits are incomplete.
- **T-1-02:** Contributors cannot safely run the repo because env requirements are undocumented, leading to ad hoc secret sharing.
- **T-1-03:** Legacy or runtime-only directories remain in ambiguous state and get recommitted later.

Mitigations required in this plan:
- Audit tracked files and runtime directories explicitly before editing docs.
- Keep only safe examples in git; never copy real values into examples.
- Document ownership and purpose of environment variables at the surface where they are used.
</threat_model>
