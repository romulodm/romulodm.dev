<threat_model>
- **T-4-01:** Generic retry defaults cause duplicate external side effects or ambiguous state transitions on critical async flows.
- **T-4-02:** Worker handlers treat retryable and non-retryable failures the same way, making failures noisy but not actually safe.
- **T-4-03:** Producer-side queue contracts and worker-side handlers drift apart on job identity, retry policy, or idempotency assumptions.

Mitigations required in this plan:
- Review shared queue defaults and per-queue overrides together instead of in isolation.
- Add targeted idempotency/retry guards for the highest-risk jobs first.
- Keep the queue contract between producers and consumers explicit and deterministic.
</threat_model>
