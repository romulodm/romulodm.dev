<threat_model>
- **T-4-04:** Jobs fail or accumulate in the queue without any operator-visible signal beyond scattered console lines.
- **T-4-05:** Worker logs remain too ad hoc to explain which queue, job, attempt, or subsystem is actually unhealthy.
- **T-4-06:** Repeatable jobs or queue consumers stop behaving correctly and the operator only notices after user-facing impact.

Mitigations required in this plan:
- Add structured worker/job logging around startup, execution, failure, and queue health.
- Add lightweight backlog and repeated-failure checks with explicit operator-visible thresholds.
- Keep the signal path local to the current codebase and architecture.
</threat_model>
